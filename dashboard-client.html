What a Mini-Contract Records

  • Who is involved: client and professional

  • Scope of work and agreed price

  • Payment milestones and dates

  • Start and expected completion dates

  • Responsibilities and cancellation terms

  • Confirmation by both parties, time-stamped

Changes create a new version, so the original terms are never silently replaced.
